A short introduction

My work spans commercial research, data systems and full-stack product delivery, with a current focus on China maritime technology.

I’m interested in the space between finding evidence and making it usable: defining the question, making relationships visible and building tools that preserve the trail back to the source.

How I approach the work.

  1. Start with the question.

    Define the decision and the evidence needed to support it.

  2. Make the structure explicit.

    Connect organizations, roles and claims without losing their context.

  3. Build for inspection.

    Keep sources traceable and uncertain claims open to review.
