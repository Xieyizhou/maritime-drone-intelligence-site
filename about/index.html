A short introduction

My work spans commercial research, data systems and full-stack product delivery, with a current focus on China maritime technology. I’m interested in the work between finding evidence and making it genuinely usable: defining the question, making relationships visible and building tools that preserve the trail back to the source.

The Maritime Partner Intelligence Console shows this approach in software. My market entry research applies it to commercial questions. I also explore new product ideas through UAV Sandbox and Job Copilot.
