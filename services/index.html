Status discipline

What can this evidence support?

  • Historical award: demand, budget or delivery precedent.
  • Operating evidence: a technology or workflow in use.
  • Forward watch: a future trigger that needs monitoring.
  • Live opportunity: an open, time-bound commercial route.

These states remain distinct throughout the research.

Paid engagement

Founding Vendor Screen

One product line and one China market-entry decision, scoped in writing before research begins.

  • 5–10 matched commercial records.
  • 3–5 buyer, prime or integrator routes.
  • Product-fit and localisation assessment.
  • Source-linked PDF and Excel deliverables.
  • A 45-minute review call and one clarification round.

The screen works toward a practical decision: proceed, partner first, monitor or deprioritise.

How it works

Start with the decision.

  1. Send your company, product URL, target mission and current China or APAC decision.
  2. Review a matched-record sample and its open question.
  3. Discuss relevance, scope and evidence availability.
  4. Agree the written scope before commissioning the full screen.

Suited to suppliers evaluating market entry, local delivery partners or a maritime use case. The service covers decision research; tender representation, bid writing, legal or tax advice and guaranteed introductions are outside its scope.
