Maritime Partner Intelligence Console

A local research workspace that turns fragmented Google Sheet records into structured leads—showing what each lead is, how the actors connect and what still needs to be investigated.

The problem

The research existed, but the relationships were hard to see.

Projects, organizations, buyers, roles, technologies, commercial states and source notes lived across multiple worksheets. Reading rows one by one made it difficult to understand what a lead represented and how the surrounding actors were connected.

The product therefore uses the project as the context boundary while preserving every source and unresolved question behind the record.

Information model

Turn rows into an inspectable lead.

The model keeps context together without flattening every organization into a permanent label.

  1. 01

    Project as context

    Buyer, organizations, roles, mission, technology and commercial status stay tied to the same project.

  2. 02

    Explicit relationships

    An organization can play different roles across different projects; the relationship is stored at project level.

  3. 03

    Source-linked observations

    Key claims retain source, date, evidence tier, verification state and original worksheet lineage.

  4. 04

    Controlled identity

    Aliases, branches and parent relationships remain separate until they have been reviewed and approved.

Outcome

A clearer view of what each lead is—and how it connects.

The workspace turns scattered clues into one inspectable project record. It helps the analyst understand the lead's context, follow the organizations and evidence around it, and see what still needs to be verified.
