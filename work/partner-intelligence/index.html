01 / Product + engineering · Case study

Make the evidence
usable, not invisible.

Maritime Partner Intelligence Console

A local workspace for understanding a lead, the organizations around it and the evidence still missing.

The problem
Research existed across worksheets, but project relationships were difficult to inspect.
My contribution
Product definition, research architecture and full-stack implementation.
The deliverable
A local analyst application, with read-only source access and an inspectable evidence trail.
01 / The problem

Rows are not the whole story.

A company name alone does not explain whether it is a buyer, prime contractor or technology provider. Those roles change from project to project.

Projects, actors, technologies and commercial states lived across multiple worksheets. The challenge was to bring them into one readable context without losing their sources or overstating uncertain relationships.

02 / Design decisions

Context before conclusions.

Start with the project

Keep the buyer, mission, technology and commercial status together. A lead remains a specific project, not a generic company profile.

Model roles explicitly

Attach each organization’s role to the project. Keep aliases and parent relationships separate until reviewed.

Preserve the trail

Retain source, date, evidence tier, verification state and worksheet lineage. An unresolved claim stays unresolved.

03 / Using the workspace

Three moves, one research thread.

  1. Filter a lead

    Use explicit product, mission, region and partner-role criteria to narrow the search. Results reflect retrieved evidence, not an AI-generated fit score.

  2. Read the relationship

    Inspect the matched project, buyer and observed organization role together, rather than treating a company’s role as permanent.

  3. Inspect the evidence

    Follow the source trail and verification state. Identify what is supported and what needs another research step.

04 / Delivery & boundaries

Built for inspection.
Not automatic judgment.

The implementation combines a FastAPI backend, SQLite/FTS5 search and a React / TypeScript interface. It connects projects, organizations and source-linked observations in a local analyst workspace.

Implemented
Deterministic search, project and organization views, evidence inspection and local research notes.
Not claimed
Automated recommendations, predictive rankings or measured improvements in business outcomes.
Private
Production data, credentials, analyst notes and commercial shortlists. There is no public hosted application.

No performance or impact metric is presented here without a reproducible measurement.
