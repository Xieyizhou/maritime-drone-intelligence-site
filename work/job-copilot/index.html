Case study updated 2 October 2026

What it does

  • Compare: show direct, partial and missing resume evidence for each requirement.
  • Prepare: draft application materials while keeping the source resume unchanged.
  • Track: organize applications and export documents locally.

How I used AI

I used AI tools to iterate on the application, then checked the workflow and outputs against the original requirements.

Try the demo

The open-source app includes a read-only demo with fictional data and no API-key requirement. Application materials remain subject to human review.
